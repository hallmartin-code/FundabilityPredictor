Deck Analyzer

Pitch Deck→Fundability

Upload a pitch deck and get a polished single-page investor PDF: the probability of closing the round in 3, 6 and 12 months, read from the deck by Claude and scored by a deterministic model.

Claude finds fields a rule-based reader cannot, and every value it returns is checked against a verbatim quote from the deck before it is accepted. Scoring stays deterministic either way.

Left unticked, both are recorded as null — meaning nobody was asked. Neither is ever recorded as consent given.

What comes back

  • One-page PDF report — probability of closing in 3, 6 and 12 months, readiness score, drivers, blockers, five prioritised actions and the TEN Capital assessment.
  • TEN Capital lead record — structured JSON including the probability bridge showing how the number was reached.
  • Extraction evidence map — all 81 fields with the page, quote and value type behind each one.

Nothing is invented. A field the deck does not state is reported as Not Provided and lowers the confidence score rather than being filled in.
